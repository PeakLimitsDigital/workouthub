FAQs

Good to know.

Are the running and HYROX plans personalised?

The digital programmes are prewritten plans built for a specific distance and experience level. For a fully individual approach, book 1:1 personal training or nutrition coaching.

How do I receive my plan?

After purchase, your digital programme is delivered straight to your email so you can start training right away.

Can I train with you in person?

Yes — 1:1 personal training runs out of Unrivaled Fitness NZ. Send a message on Instagram to check availability and book a consult.

How does personalised nutrition work?

You'll complete a detailed intake and suitability review first. Once approved, you'll receive your framework or start weekly coaching check-ins.

What if I have an injury or medical condition?

Please get in touch before purchasing. Some situations need individual review or clearance from a health professional before starting a programme.
